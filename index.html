01 / How it works

Three parts.
One broadcast.

AA station is a playlist

Bring a Spotify playlist and it becomes a station. The music is the programming.

BEveryone hears the same thing

One persistent cursor drives each station. When the track changes, it changes for everyone listening. Not personal playback. Radio.

CChat on the air

Live chat sits in the same room as the music. The crowd is part of the broadcast.

02 / The engine

No servers.

Cloudflare-native

  • Durable Objects — one per station. It owns the now-playing cursor, the alarm that advances it, and the chat.
  • Workers — the API. No processes to babysit.
  • D1 — stations, users, chat history.
  • Svelte 5 — the player, on Pages.
